Installed

  1. Connect the scanner by USB, switch it on and insert a film holder (front door closed).
  2. Open Image Capture, choose DiMAGE Scan Dual IV, and scan. Film type and negative handling are set at localhost:8090.
  3. Or open Terminal and type dsdual4 for the full menu.

Turn the background service off with dsdual4 service stop. Uninstall with sudo /usr/local/share/dsdual4/uninstall.sh.
