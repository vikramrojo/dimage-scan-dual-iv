dsd4

Driver for the Konica Minolta DiMAGE Scan Dual IV on macOS 11 and later, Apple silicon and Intel. No kernel extension.

@DOWNLOAD_LABEL@ @RELEASE_NOTE@ · Releases · Source

Raw DNG

Linear scanner data with Konica Minolta's colour calibration, at the size of a 16-bit TIFF (88 MB a frame). Nothing is baked in: white balance, exposure and inversion stay adjustable in Lightroom, darktable or Negative Lab Pro.

Multi-sampled and HDR scans are stored as float, which keeps fine tonal steps in dense areas where a 16-bit TIFF has few. Negatives can also be saved as positive DNGs that match the Minolta TIFF in Preview.

Colour profiles

Slides embed MLTF3200p.icc, Minolta-processed negatives MLTF3200n.icc: the original software's profiles.

HDR

A second pass 2 stops brighter, aligned to the first and merged. In simulation, noise in the densest areas drops about 4×: up to 2 more stops of usable range in slide shadows and negative highlights. The file stays 88 MB. Works with Minolta processing.

Negatives

  • Minolta processing: the original auto-exposure and film curves.
  • Simple invert: in density, orange mask removed.
  • Raw: left negative, for Negative Lab Pro.

Autofocus

About a third of the original's lens moves. --af classic runs the original sequence.

Deblur

Reduces camera blur from a missed focus. The blur is measured per region; sharp frames are left alone, and grain and colour are kept.

dsd4 scan --deblur low -o d.tiff
dsd4 deblur scan.dng --level high

4000 dpi Experimental

Undoes the scanner's own measured blur, reduces sensor and colour noise, and resamples to 4000 dpi. Grain is not sharpened. Based on Norman Koren's film-scanner MTF studies.

Razor-blade edgeMTF50
3200 dpi scan44.8 lp/mm
Resampled to 4000 by macOS35.3 lp/mm
--experimental 4000dpi53.8 lp/mm

Uses a typical Dual IV's measurements unless you calibrate yours:

dsd4 research mtf     # razor blade in a slide mount
dsd4 research grain   # frame with sky or blank film
dsd4 research noise   # blank film

Install

  1. Open @DOWNLOAD_FILE@.
  2. Connect the scanner and insert a holder.
  3. Scan from Image Capture or Preview, or run dsd4 in Terminal.

Command line

dsd4
dsd4 scan --dpi 3200 -o s.tiff
dsd4 scan --film nega --minolta -o n.tiff
dsd4 scan --format dng --samples 4 -o r.dng
dsd4 scan --format dng --hdr -o h.dng
dsd4 scan-all --frames 4 -o roll
dsd4 --help
